# Upwork Freelancers by Country and Technology Stack

*Research date: 14 September 2026. Coverage: 191 individually named sovereign states/economies after exclusion of prohibited or operationally unsupported locations.*

## Executive summary

Upwork does **not** publish an auditable country-by-country inventory of freelancer profiles, nor a country-by-technology matrix. Its current eligibility page says only that the platform supports freelancers in “180+ countries” and names specific restrictions; its annual demand reports rank skills from completed jobs and freelancer earnings but do not disclose freelancer headcounts ([Upwork eligibility policy](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211067778-Who-s-eligible-to-join-and-use-Upwork), [Upwork 2025 skills methodology](https://www.upwork.com/research/in-demand-skills-2025), [Upwork 2026 skills release](https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upworks-demand-skills-2026-demand-top-ai-skills-more-doubles-ai)). Accordingly, any table claiming exact Upwork freelancer counts for 100+ countries and stacks without account-level search data is not verifiable.

This report therefore separates three things: **(1)** official availability policy, **(2)** direct Upwork demand evidence, and **(3)** a transparent country-level technology-supply proxy computed from the official 2024 Stack Overflow Developer Survey archive. The proxy covers 57,232 responses; 191 countries/economies are listed, including zero-observation rows after normalization; the underlying survey reported 65,437 coders from 185 countries before exclusions ([Stack Overflow 2024 survey](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2024/), [official data archive](https://github.com/StackExchange/Survey/tree/main/packages/archive/2024)). It is a developer-population signal—not an Upwork freelancer count.

Across the included proxy sample, the most prevalent tracked stacks were DevOps/cloud (45,093 respondents), JavaScript/TypeScript (38,125 respondents), Python (28,896 respondents), Node.js (18,756 respondents), React (18,133 respondents). The least prevalent were Swift (2,689), Ruby/Rails (3,322), Flutter (4,075), Kotlin (5,311), WordPress (5,390). These counts are non-exclusive because one person can report multiple technologies ([official data archive](https://github.com/StackExchange/Survey/tree/main/packages/archive/2024)).

On Upwork itself, the 2026 demand list ranks full-stack development first in Coding & Web Development, followed by web design, front-end development, mobile app development, and back-end development; in Data Science & Analytics, data analytics ranks first, followed by data extraction and machine learning ([Upwork 2026 skills release](https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upworks-demand-skills-2026-demand-top-ai-skills-more-doubles-ai)). Fastest-growing technology-related skills included AI integration (+178%), AI chatbot development (+71%), AI data annotation and labeling (+154%), knowledge representation (+31%), and generative AI modeling (+21%), based on 2025 freelancer earnings from completed U.S.-originated jobs and a $100,000 minimum aggregate-earnings threshold ([Upwork 2026 skills release](https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upworks-demand-skills-2026-demand-top-ai-skills-more-doubles-ai)).

## 1. Registration availability: what is official, and what is inferred

Upwork's authoritative public statement is not a full country roster: it says the service supports freelancers in more than 180 countries, while warning that it is not available everywhere and that additional legal restrictions can change ([Upwork eligibility policy](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211067778-Who-s-eligible-to-join-and-use-Upwork)). The same page states that Russia and Belarus have been suspended since 1 May 2022, and prohibits accounts or use by businesses or individuals located or traveling in Iran, North Korea, Crimea, Cuba, the Luhansk People's Republic, the Donetsk People's Republic, plus any other country or region restricted by law; it also applies U.S. Specially Designated Nationals restrictions ([Upwork eligibility policy](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211067778-Who-s-eligible-to-join-and-use-Upwork), [Russia/Belarus suspension](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/5691956088339-Suspended-operations-in-Russia-and-Belarus)).

## 2. Methodology

### 2.1 Country technology proxy

The official 2024 Stack Overflow archive provides response-level `Country` and multi-select fields for languages, web frameworks, platforms, miscellaneous technology, and tools ([official data archive](https://github.com/StackExchange/Survey/tree/main/packages/archive/2024)). For every included country, this analysis counts respondents using each defined stack:

- **JavaScript/TypeScript**: either language; **Python**, **PHP**, **Java**, **Go**, **Swift**, and **Kotlin**: exact language selections.
- **React**, **Node.js**, and **WordPress**: exact web-framework selections.
- **.NET/C#**: C#, Visual Basic (.NET), ASP.NET, ASP.NET Core, Blazor, .NET 5+, .NET Framework, or .NET MAUI.
- **Ruby/Rails**: Ruby or Ruby on Rails; **Flutter**: exact miscellaneous-technology selection.
- **Data science/ML**: at least one of Apache Spark, CUDA, Hadoop, Hugging Face Transformers, JAX, Keras, NumPy, OpenCV, Pandas, scikit-learn, TensorFlow, Tidyverse, Torch/PyTorch, or MLflow.
- **DevOps/cloud**: at least one named cloud platform, or Ansible, Chef, Docker, Kubernetes, Podman, Pulumi, Puppet, or Terraform.

Counts are non-exclusive. “Most frequent” means the three largest proxy counts in a country. “Unused” means zero observations in that country's survey sample—not zero real professionals. Where every stack has at least one observation, “least” means the three smallest non-zero counts. Data-quality labels are deterministic: medium proxy sample at n ≥ 200; low at 50–199; very low below 50.

### 2.2 Why no modeled Upwork headcount is presented

Upwork's public hire landing pages expose highlighted profiles but no exhaustive “freelancers found” total in the retrievable page content. Checks of country-skill pages for [JavaScript in India](https://www.upwork.com/hire/javascript-developers/in/), [Python in Germany](https://www.upwork.com/hire/python-developers/de/), [React in the United States](https://www.upwork.com/hire/react-js-developers/us/), [WordPress in the Philippines](https://www.upwork.com/hire/wordpress-developers/ph/), [Java in Pakistan](https://www.upwork.com/hire/java-developers/pk/), [data science in Ukraine](https://www.upwork.com/hire/data-scientists/ua/), and [DevOps in the United Kingdom](https://www.upwork.com/hire/devops-engineers/gb/) did not reveal total-result counts in the public page text. Multiplying external country shares by an obsolete platform-wide “registered users” figure would manufacture false precision, so the `upwork_freelancer_count` field is explicitly “Not publicly disclosed.”

### 2.3 External validity

The Oxford Internet Institute's Online Labour Index (OLI) is useful for geography but is not Upwork-specific: its 2017 worker supplement covered Fiverr, Freelancer, Guru, and PeoplePerHour, estimated those four platforms had roughly 3.5 million registered users and about 100,000 workers active in a typical week, and defined “currently active” as completing a project within 28 days ([OII methodology](https://ilabour.oii.ox.ac.uk/measuring-the-supply-of-digital-labour-how-the-oli-worker-supplement-is-constructed/)). In a July 2017 snapshot, India represented 24% of observed workers, Bangladesh 16%, and the United States 12%; the Indian subcontinent supplied 55% of software-development-and-technology work, while large Eastern European countries were also strongest in software development ([OII geography analysis](https://ilabour.oii.ox.ac.uk/where-are-online-workers-located-the-international-division-of-digital-gig-work/)). These figures provide directional validation for large South Asian and Eastern European supply pools but must not be read as current Upwork shares.

The World Bank's broader 2023 estimate of 154 million–435 million online gig workers worldwide—up to 12% of the global labor force—covers a much wider platform universe and likewise cannot be converted into Upwork headcounts ([World Bank, *Working Without Borders*](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/entities/publication/ebc4a7e2-85c6-467b-8713-e2d77e954c6c)).

### Interpreting country patterns

- The country leader distribution is DevOps/cloud: 128 countries, JavaScript/TypeScript: 23 countries, No country observations in proxy: 18 countries, .NET/C#: 13 countries, Data science/ML: 4 countries, Python: 3 countries, Flutter: 1 countries, Kotlin: 1 countries. - Large-sample countries such as the United States, Germany, India, the United Kingdom, Ukraine, France, Canada, Poland, the Netherlands, Brazil, Italy, Australia, Spain, and Sweden support more stable within-country comparisons than countries with fewer than 50 respondents ([official data archive](https://github.com/StackExchange/Survey/tree/main/packages/archive/2024)).
- “Unused” results should be interpreted as **sample zeros**. They are especially common in small countries because rare stacks such as Ruby/Rails, Flutter, Swift, or Kotlin may simply not appear among a handful of respondents.
- Upwork demand and developer supply are not the same concept. Upwork's skill rankings use completed jobs and freelancer earnings with U.S.-origin demand; this analysis uses self-reported technologies from developers worldwide ([Upwork 2025 methodology](https://www.upwork.com/research/in-demand-skills-2025), [official Stack Overflow archive](https://github.com/StackExchange/Survey/tree/main/packages/archive/2024)).

## 5. Limitations and decision guidance

1. **No public census.** Upwork does not publish country-by-stack freelancer totals, and public hire pages are marketing/landing pages rather than reproducible census endpoints ([Upwork eligibility policy](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211067778-Who-s-eligible-to-join-and-use-Upwork), [Upwork India JavaScript page](https://www.upwork.com/hire/javascript-developers/in/)).
2. **Availability is inferred.** The country roster is not an official Upwork list; each country should be verified during account creation or via Upwork support, especially where payments, identity verification, export controls, or local law may intervene ([Upwork eligibility policy](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211067778-Who-s-eligible-to-join-and-use-Upwork)).
3. **Proxy selection bias.** Stack Overflow respondents are developers, not necessarily freelancers or Upwork members; country sample sizes vary sharply ([Stack Overflow 2024 survey](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2024/)).
4. **Technology taxonomy overlap.** Stack categories overlap by design; a single respondent may count toward JavaScript/TypeScript, React, Node.js, and DevOps/cloud.
5. **Time mismatch.** Supply proxy data are from 2024, while demand evidence is from 2025 activity reported in 2026 ([official Stack Overflow archive](https://github.com/StackExchange/Survey/tree/main/packages/archive/2024), [Upwork 2026 skills release](https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upworks-demand-skills-2026-demand-top-ai-skills-more-doubles-ai)).
6. **Country naming.** Survey labels were normalized, including two Korea labels and legacy names. Hong Kong, Kosovo, Isle of Man, Palestine, and Taiwan are retained as separately reported economies/locations; this is a data-coverage choice, not a political-status judgment.

For sourcing decisions, use the table to identify plausible stack-country pools, then validate actual Upwork supply with a logged-in talent search or Upwork's enterprise/API access. Procurement should prioritize countries with medium proxy samples, corroborate skill depth through work history and tests, and treat very-low-sample countries as discovery leads rather than quantified markets.
